Features

Everything a connected drive needs

Built local-first: once phones find each other, the awareness network keeps working with no signal at all.

Nearby vehicle detection

Phones discover and connect directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, then share approximate position, speed and heading ~once a second.

Merge requests

Signal a left or right merge to nearby drivers. It is a courtesy request — never treated as permission.

Verified emergency alerts

Ambulance, police and fire vehicles are verified by an administrator and prove it with a signed credential. Fakes are ignored.

Hazards & accidents

Report accidents, obstructions, breakdowns, road damage and closures. Nearby drivers are warned within seconds.

Collision-risk awareness

Trajectory and time-based reasoning that respects GPS accuracy. Proximity alone never raises an alarm.

Maps & navigation

Search a destination, preview the route and hand off to turn-by-turn. Navigation works with or without the network.

How it works

From sign-up to connected in minutes

  1. 1

    Register your vehicle

    Create an account and add your vehicle. The server issues a permanent DriveLink ID such as DL-IND-000001.

  2. 2

    Start a journey

    Pick a destination, confirm, and the app switches to a minimal driving dashboard with voice alerts.

  3. 3

    Phones link up

    Nearby DriveLink phones authenticate each other with signed messages and share live status — directly, no internet.

Privacy & safety

Designed to be trustworthy

Your account lives in the cloud. Your live location does not.

  • Live positions are exchanged only phone-to-phone under a rotating session ID.
  • Registration numbers never leave your account; strict row-level security protects every table.
  • Every message is signed, rate-limited, expiry-checked and replay-protected.

Experimental, not certified

DriveLink is a driver-awareness aid. It is not an ADAS and does not control your vehicle. Smartphone GPS is not lane-accurate. Never operate your phone while driving.

About DriveLink

Connect. Communicate. Navigate.

DriveLink lets Android phones in nearby vehicles exchange approximate position, heading and alerts directly over Google Nearby Connections — no internet needed once peers connect. This site manages your account and vehicles and offers a trajectory simulator.
